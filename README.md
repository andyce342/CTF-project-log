PROJECT P1: CTF Sprint

Goal: Build real Linux, crypto, and problem-solving fundamentals by solving a curated set of legal CTF challenges, and prove it with a write-up log. The write-up log is the deliverable, not the flags. 

Challenge lists: 
Track A: OverTheWire Bandit, levels 0 → 15 
Track B: picoGym starter set (do ≥ 8 in total, from at least 3 of the four categories) 

Write-up log: one entry per solved challenge
     1) Challenge name & category
     2) What it asked
     3) What I tried, in order
     4) What actually worked: the technique/command (describe it; you don't need to paste the flag)
     5) The concept I learned
     6) If I used my assistant: what I asked and what hint it gave (Socratic: no answers); At least one entry that shows the                  guardrail working: a hint you got, not an answer

Acceptance criteria: 
1) Bandit 0 → 15 reached (or a documented blocker at the level you stopped)
2) ≥ 8 picoGym challenges across ≥ 3 categories
3) A write-up entry for every solved challenge
4) At least one entry that shows the guardrail working: a hint you got, not an answer
